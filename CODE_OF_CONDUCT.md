# Code of conduct

Be respectful, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and give
constructive feedback about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, threats and
publishing private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation to protect
contributors. Report concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Reports are handled confidentially where possible, with a proportionate
response and an opportunity for clarification.

The project follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
