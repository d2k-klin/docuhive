# Synthetic identifiers

Only invented `QA Utility 01` … `QA Utility 15`, `alice`, `bob`, addresses ending
in `@example.invalid`, and document markers `QA-DOC:case-001` … `case-015` are used.
There are no phone numbers, postal addresses, IBANs or personal names.
Dates and amounts are generated ground truth, not copied from an archive.
Any additional identity needs an exact allow-list entry and a hygiene test.
